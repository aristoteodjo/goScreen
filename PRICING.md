## What every plan includes

- The full studio: infinite canvas, scene composition, synced devices, live web pages and Android
  emulators / devices over ADB.
- Recording of a device, the canvas or the scene, with webcam, microphone, automatic voice cleanup,
  pause / resume, per-device takes and automatic site tours.
- The editor: timeline, split / trim / speed, auto-zooms, touch rings and tap sounds, text, image, video and
  music overlays, webcam overlay or split screen, blur and pixelate boxes, MP4 export.

## Plan details

### Free
For trying goScreen and personal projects. Up to 3 projects, exports up to 720p with a goScreen watermark,
standard device frames (iPhone 17, iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone Air, iPhone 16e, iPad mini, Pixel 10, Pixel 9a,
Galaxy S25 and S25 Ultra, Galaxy Tab S10+, MacBooks, iMac, Windows laptop, monitor, generic Android).

### Pro: $19 / month or $149 / year
For videos that go to customers, app stores, landing pages and social networks.

- Unlimited projects

- **Premium devices**: iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPad Pro 11", iPad Air 13", Pixel 10 Pro XL, and future premium packs

- **Cloud sync** of projects between your machines
- **Share links** to send a video for review without exporting it

### Team: contact us

brand assets in one place.

### Enterprise: contact us

## FAQ

**Is the source code available?**
Yes, the code is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/aristoteodjo/goScreen). The hosted plans pay for
cloud features, premium content, ongoing device updates and support.

**Can I cancel at any time?**
Yes. Monthly plans stop at the end of the current month; yearly plans at the end of the current year. Your
projects and exported videos stay yours.

**What happens to my projects if I go back to Free?**
Nothing is deleted. Projects beyond the Free limit become read-only until you upgrade again or remove some.
